## Data and ideas

- **Trick-or-treat:** all streets, houses, rewards, and routes are generated by the included code. No real addresses or personal data are used.
- **Stable matching:** synthetic preference lists and an implementation of the deferred-acceptance algorithm introduced by David Gale and Lloyd Shapley, “College Admissions and the Stability of Marriage,” 1962. [Original paper DOI](https://doi.org/10.2307/2312726).
- **Job-search reference line:** U.S. Bureau of Labor Statistics, Current Population Survey, median duration of unemployment (weeks), seasonally adjusted. Distributed through the Federal Reserve Bank of St. Louis as [FRED series UEMPMED](https://fred.stlouisfed.org/series/UEMPMED). The included data were retrieved 2026-09-01 and end at July 2026. BLS-produced data are in the public domain; attribution does not imply agency endorsement. [BLS copyright statement](https://www.bls.gov/bls/linksite.htm).
- **Odyssey:** literary counts and traditional place identifications from Homer's Odyssey, interpreted by the figure's ship accounting. The flow-map approach is inspired by Charles Joseph Minard; no reproduction of Minard's original drawing or modern translated poem text is bundled.
- **Coastline:** [Natural Earth](https://www.naturalearthdata.com/), 1:10m land and minor islands. [Public-domain terms](https://www.naturalearthdata.com/about/terms-of-use/). The source-site build clips the geometry to 8–28.5°E and 31.5–42.5°N, simplifies it to 900m, and drops islands smaller than 3km². The included compiled file has 168 rings and 4,424 vertices.
